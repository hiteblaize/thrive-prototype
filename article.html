Why Your Habits Today Outlast the Medication

Most people who stop GLP-1 medications gain most of the weight back within a year. Not because they failed. Because they stopped without the right foundation underneath them.

Carda was built to change that outcome. What you are doing right now — on Day 14 — is the beginning of that foundation.

An 18-month plan, not just a prescription

Carda is a structured three-phase program that runs alongside your medication — and continues long after it stops. Each phase has a specific job. And the habits you build in Phase 1 determine how well Phases 2 and 3 go.

Your three phases
1
Protect while you lose — now
Your GLP-1 is suppressing appetite and accelerating fat loss. The risk is that without resistance training and protein, up to 40% of what you lose can be muscle, not fat. Phase 1 makes sure the weight coming off is the right kind.
2
Stop the medication safely — 9 weeks
When you reach your goal weight, most programs just stop the drug. We don't. Over 9 weeks we taper your dose to zero while increasing coaching and exercise. Your hunger returns as the drug fades — we are there for exactly that moment.
3
Stay there — 12 months
Off the medication, the habits take over. Daily movement, ongoing strength training, and group support replace the drug's appetite effect. This is where the program pays off — not as a pill you take, but as a body that manages itself.
Where you are right now

Day 14. Two weeks into Phase 1. The check-ins, protein goals, and sessions with Coach Haley are not busywork. They are the exact inputs that determine whether Phase 2 goes smoothly and whether Phase 3 sticks.

Nothing you do today is small. It is foundation.

Key takeaways
Most patients regain weight after stopping GLP-1s — Carda's three phases prevent that
Phase 1 protects muscle while the medication works
Phase 2 tapers the drug over 9 weeks — not a sudden stop
The habits you build now are what make stopping possible
Today's Microstep
Write down one thing that feels easier than two weeks ago
After your session today, take 60 seconds. One thing — carrying groceries, climbing stairs, getting up from a chair. That is your evidence. That is the fire staying lit.
